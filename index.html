04 / PROFILE

THE PERSON BEHIND THE MUSIC

From a few
words to a
finished song.

The starting point is usually a lyric, a melody, or a feeling that needs a sound.

Bhavesh writes primarily in Hindi with Urdu influences, and also writes in Marathi. His independent practice spans songwriting, composition, arrangement, vocal recording and direction, production, mixing and mastering in FL Studio.

He is based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, and is open to full-time music roles, paid internships and artist collaborations.
